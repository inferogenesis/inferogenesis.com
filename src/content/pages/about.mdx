## What Inferogenesis is

Inferogenesis builds open-source software for continuous active inference, and the
research that tests it. [cpomdp](/projects/cpomdp/) is the library. It builds agents from
linear-Gaussian generative models, with Kalman-filter perception and actions chosen by
expected free energy. [warrantlib](/projects/warrantlib/) is the vocabulary its checks
report in. Each claim says how well it is warranted, apart from whether it held.
