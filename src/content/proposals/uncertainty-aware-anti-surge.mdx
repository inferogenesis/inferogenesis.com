## From the published method to code

[Sathish et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1115/GT2024-124905) set out a ten-step method
for designing an active inference anti-surge controller, and outline a controller in
pymdp. The table maps each step to what cpomdp v0.5.0 already has.

- **DP:** a differential-pressure flow meter, such as an orifice plate.
- **Λ:** the weight on the economic preference. It sets where the agent wants to sit.
- **PID:** proportional-integral-derivative, the standard industrial controller.
- **MPC:** model predictive control.

The cpomdp results are direction results on a simplified plant. The plant has no
compressor dynamics and no noise, and the agent plans one step ahead. The size of the
standoff from surge depends on Λ, so it is not a citable number. The "Open" rows are
the proposed study.
